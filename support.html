Support

We're here to help.

Support for both Ether & Ink apps — Bibliomancy (the reading tracker) and Lore Night (book trivia with friends). General help is below; app-specific answers are grouped by app.

Contact Us

For bug reports, feature requests, account issues, or general questions about either app, the quickest way to reach us is the feedback form — it opens your email app with your message ready to send:

💬 Open the Feedback Form →

Prefer email? Write to us directly at admin@etherink.net. We read every message and aim to reply within a couple of days.

Data & Privacy Requests

To request a data export, account deletion, or to exercise your privacy rights under GDPR/CCPA:

  • Bibliomancy — delete: Go to Settings → Delete Cloud Data
  • Bibliomancy — export: Go to Settings → Export Library (JSON or CSV)
  • Lore Night: Game rooms and answers are automatically deleted after 24 hours, so there's nothing to export or purge.
  • By email: admin@etherink.net

We never sell your personal data. See our Privacy Policy for the full details of how we handle your data.

Bibliomancy — Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?
No. Bibliomancy works fully offline with no account. Signing in is optional and only needed for cloud backup and community features.

How do I back up my library?
Two options: (1) sign in with Google or email for automatic cloud backup, or (2) export your library as JSON/CSV from Settings.

Can I delete my data?
Yes. Local data is deleted when you uninstall. Cloud data can be deleted from Settings → Delete Cloud Data. You can also email us for complete account deletion.

What age is required, and why is the app 13+?
Anyone can use the core app. The offline reading tracker needs no account and collects no personal data, so readers of any age can catalog books, log pages, keep streaks, and cast the Oracle. The 13+ requirement applies only to the community and cloud features — leaderboards, reading parties, reviews, shared quotes, and cloud backup/sync — because those create an account and share content with other people. Under children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and the GDPR children's provisions (EU/UK), we choose not to collect personal data from children under 13, so we gate those social features to ages 13 and up rather than collect kids' data. Younger readers can still enjoy the full offline experience; the age check happens on first launch and an under-13 choice is remembered across restarts.

How do reading parties work?
Create or join a party reading the same book. Pick a reading class, complete quests together, and fill the Adventure Bar to complete the adventure.

Lore Night — Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lore Night?
A live, multiplayer book-trivia game and a companion experience to Bibliomancy. Play right in your browser at lorenight.net.

Do I need an account?
No. You can jump in as a guest with a wizard name. Signing in with your Bibliomancy account is optional and lets your profile carry over.

How do I play?
Host a room and share the join code with friends, join a friend's room with their code, or play a solo round. Answer themed trivia questions and race for points.

What happens to my game data?
Game rooms and answers are automatically deleted after 24 hours. Leaderboard standings persist.

Can I add my own questions?
Yes. Use the "Submit a Question" option in Lore Night to suggest trivia for the community.

What age is required?
You must be 13 or older to play Lore Night. Because it's a live multiplayer game with shared display names and community leaderboards, the same community-features rule applies — see why the apps are 13+.
